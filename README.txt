Give Homey a body. Reachy Mini Controller turns a Reachy Mini robot into the Eyes, Ears, and Voice of your smart home.

VOICE
Reachy speaks any text through its speaker, and can announce the time or the current time and weather (cuckoo-clock style, using your Homey location). Choose your voice provider: Google Translate for free, or bring an OpenAI or ElevenLabs API key for natural, high-quality speech. Its antennas wiggle as it talks.

EYES
Get a Flow trigger when Reachy notices a person and turns to look at them. (Requires a face-tracking app running on the robot.)

EARS
Get a Flow trigger when Reachy hears someone speak, including the direction the sound came from (front, left, right, or behind).

TWO-WAY CONVERSATION
Switch Reachy into a live spoken conversation using its on-board conversation app — talk to it and it talks back, no API key required. When conversation ends, Reachy stays awake and upright instead of slumping into standby.

EXPRESSIVE MOTION
Make Reachy nod, shake its head, look around, express emotions (happy, sad, curious, excited, confused), or turn toward the last sound it heard — straight from your Flows.

CONTROL YOUR HOME BY VOICE
With the optional conversation tool, say things like "Reachy, turn off the living room lights" or "make the kitchen blue," and Reachy controls your Homey devices — lights (on/off, brightness, colour, scenes), TVs and speakers (on/off, volume, mute, play/pause), and more — then confirms out loud. A "Reachy received a command" trigger lets you build Flows for anything else.

REQUIREMENTS
A Reachy Mini on the same network as your Homey Pro. Optional: an OpenAI or ElevenLabs key for premium voices; a face-tracking app on the robot for the Eyes trigger; the conversation app for two-way voice and home control.
